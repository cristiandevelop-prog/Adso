## Protagonists: Lucía Caminos and Jason Duval, dual protagonists, character switching

### Takeaway
Rockstar markets GTA VI as a two-protagonist, Bonnie-and-Clyde-style story set in Leonida. The official site gives Lucía a prison backstory. Character switching between the two is confirmed in gameplay: Rockstar's extended-look footage shows it, and reviews and guides describe it after launch. Rockstar's own published description of the switching rules was not found in my sources.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFICIAL – rockstargames.com/VI]** Lucía's official profile: fighting for her family landed her in the Leonida Penitentiary, and "sheer luck" got her out. "Lucia's learned her lesson — only smart moves from here." It frames a life with Jason as "her way out". — [Search summary citing Rockstar's VI site](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/only-in-leonida); [Grand Theft Wiki](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Lucia_Caminos)
- **[OFICIAL – Trailer 1, Dec 2023]** Trailer 1 opens with Lucía in a prison uniform behind chain-link fencing. — [GTA Fandom](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Lucia_Caminos)

Rockstar updated the site with 70 screenshots, character profiles, map locations and artwork. — [Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/new-grand-theft-auto-vi-trailer-highlights-bonnie-clyde-style-story); [Gematsu, Trailer 2 characters and places](https://www.gematsu.com/2025/05/grand-theft-auto-vi-second-trailer-characters-and-places-detailed); [GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/articles/gta-6-trailer-2-is-out-now); [AV Club](https://www.avclub.com/grand-theft-auto-vi-second-trailer)

## The state of Leonida and its regions

### Takeaway
Rockstar's official site names six areas of Leonida, its fictional version of Florida: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. Each has a short official description. The descriptions below reach me second-hand through sites that reproduce Rockstar's copy.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFICIAL, per secondary reproductions]** The six named regions are Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. — [Mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/gamebase/guides/grand-theft-auto-6-interactive-map); [leonida.vc](https://leonida.vc/regions); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_VI)
- **Grassrivers:** a vast subtropical wetland of swamps, mangroves and cypress, Leonida's take on the Everglades. The Everglades comparison is media interpretation. — [GTA Boss](https://www.gtaboss.gg/gta-6/guides/gta-6-map); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_VI)
- **Port Gellhorn:** a Gulf-coast port town "the tourists left behind", with a rough new economy. — [FMTweak (reproduces Rockstar copy)](https://fmtweak.com/gta-6-map-leonida/)
- **Ambrosia:** the agricultural and industrial heartland. The Allied Crystal sugar refinery and a local biker gang are named. — [gamecryst](https://gamecryst.com/gta-6/locations/leonida/); [gta6everything](https://www.gta6everything.com/locations)
- **Mount Kalaga National Park:** a northern wilderness and national landmark, with hunting, fishing and off-road trails. — [wikigta6](https://www.wikigta6.com/gta-6-map2/); [state-of-leonida](https://state-of-leonida.com/en/map)
- **Vice City:** the launch trailer and pre-launch coverage present the game as "a return to Vice City". — [Enstarz](https://www.enstarz.com/articles/246660/20261118/gta-6-release-date-platforms-preload-details.htm)
- **[OFICIAL page exists]** Rockstar's VI site has a page titled "Only in Leonida". — [rockstargames.com/VI/only-in-leonida](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/only-in-leonida)

### Inferences
- A "6 regiones de Leonida" explainer is safe content because all six names are official. Real-Florida parallels such as the Everglades, the Florida Keys and the Panhandle are commentary, not Rockstar statements.

### Gaps
- No official Leonida Keys description was retrieved; the Florida Keys parallel is implied.
- Map size has not been confirmed by Rockstar.
- One fan site claims an official printable map is added at launch, while other sources say there is no official map. This is unconfirmed.
